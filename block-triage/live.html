New areas (drawn, split, or combined) are automatically queued to be added as MapRoulette tasks - nothing is created until you process the queue below, one area at a time. Use "Add now" on an area's popup to create it immediately instead.
"Remove task from challenge" queues the delete instead of doing it right away — nothing is deleted until you process the queue below, one task at a time.
Editing an area's boundary (via "Edit boundary…" on a linked area's popup) queues the MapRoulette side of it too - the old task gets deleted and a new one created with the edited shape, once you process the queue below.
Splitting happens locally right away - the resulting pieces show an orange dashed outline and can each be dropped ("Drop this piece" in its popup, at least one must remain) until you process the queue below, which syncs it to MapRoulette: deletes the old task (if it was task-linked) and creates a new one for every piece still standing, one split at a time.
"Replace areas…" queues the swap instead of syncing it right away - the replacement areas show a teal dashed outline until you process the queue below, which deletes any linked MapRoulette tasks for the areas you replaced and creates new ones for the areas you drew instead, one group at a time.
